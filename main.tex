\section{Paper Structure}
The paper should have a maximum length of 6 pages, including: Abstract, Appendix (if needed) and References.

The main text shall be in Arial, size 10.

Section headings are numbered (except for Abstract, Keywords, Nomenclature, Acronyms/Abbreviations, Acknowledgements, Appendix and References).

All section headings are bold (use format provided in the template as "Title 3").

You are free to choose the structure of your paper, but some sections should be present: Introduction, Discussion and Conclusions. Between Introduction and Discussion feel free to include the sections you see more suitable, for example, State-of-the-art, Methodology, Results, etc.
